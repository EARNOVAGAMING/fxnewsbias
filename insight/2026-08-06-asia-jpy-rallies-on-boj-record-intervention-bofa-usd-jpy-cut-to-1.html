JPY Rallies on BoJ Record Intervention & BofA USD/JPY Cut to 149

Asia session is opening — here is the overnight forex sentiment picture as Tokyo, Singapore and Sydney desks come online. Japanese Yen (JPY) leads forex sentiment today with a strong bullish reading. Here is what drove the move and what to watch next.

What Happened

The Japanese Yen rallied sharply during the Asia session after authorities deployed record intervention to defend the currency, pushing USD/JPY back toward mid-May levels. This aggressive policy action—reported by FXStreet and ForexLive—underscored the Bank of Japan's commitment to arresting yen weakness and signalled that policymakers remain willing to step in at unfavorable levels. The intervention itself became the session's primary catalyst, reassuring traders that downside protection exists.

BofA subsequently cut its year-end USD/JPY forecast to 149, down from prior guidance, implying material yen appreciation over the remainder of 2026. That revision, combined with the tangible proof of official support, shifted near-term sentiment decisively in JPY's favour. Weakness in US employment data—ADP print of just 44k jobs—further undercut the dollar narrative, reducing the probability of near-term Federal Reserve rate hikes and narrowing the interest-rate differential that had been pressuring the yen. The exchange rate backdrop now favours carry-trade unwinds and safe-haven inflows.

Market Reaction

The broader forex market repriced risk-off positioning as the weak US labor print collided with the yen's official support. USD/JPY became the session's dominant pair, with the dollar index falling to its lowest levels in weeks amid downward pressure across all major crosses. The US Dollar sentiment collapsed to just 35/100 (bearish)—a 33-point gap versus JPY—highlighting the starkest divergence on the board. Euro and Australian Dollar pairs benefited from dollar selling, though neither currency developed meaningful upside without fundamental anchors of their own.

Technically, USD/JPY price action confirmed that intervention has established a near-term floor; any bounce toward 151 will now face resistance from fresh official selling. The currency strength of the yen extended to emerging-market peers—South Korean Won traders cited intervention chatter—suggesting broader Asian policymaker concern about depreciation spirals. Safe-haven flows into yen were tempered slightly by global risk-on sentiment following US-Iran Hormuz de-escalation talks, but official support trumped that dynamic.

What's Driving the Move

Three key threads run through the bullish Japanese Yen story:

  1. Bank of Japan deployed record intervention to arrest yen weakness, signalling sustained policymaker determination to defend the currency at unfavorable levels
  2. BofA's revised USD/JPY year-end forecast of 149 implies 2.5% yen appreciation from current levels, reshaping capital-flows outlook for carry trades
  3. Weak ADP employment print (44k) reduced Fed rate-hike conviction, narrowing the interest-rate advantage that had compressed yen valuations

What to Watch Next

📈 Bull case for the move
If USD/JPY closes the session below 150, technical momentum will confirm the intervention floor and likely attract algorithmic buy stops into mid-149. Any fresh dovish commentary from Fed officials—or a sub-100k print on Friday's nonfarm payroll—would accelerate yen strength toward 147–148 and potentially trigger a carry-trade unwind spiral that feeds the rally.
📉 Risk to the view
Should crude oil prices stabilise and risk appetite reignite sharply, the yen's safe-haven bid could evaporate, and the interest-rate carry advantage could reassert itself if Fed expectations stabilise above three 2026 rate cuts. Additionally, BNY Mellon flagged that passive foreign interest in Japanese assets remains thin, limiting organic inflow support if intervention quiets—a scenario that would leave USD/JPY vulnerable to reversal toward 152–153.

Watch the London and New York open for fresh dollar positioning and any additional BoJ commentary that might either reinforce or dial back intervention signals heading into the weekend.
