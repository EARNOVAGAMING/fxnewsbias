CHF Rallies on Fed Rate-Hike Fade & Hormuz Safe-Haven Bid

London is opening, here is the forex sentiment setup heading into the European session. Swiss Franc (CHF) leads forex sentiment today with a strong bullish reading. Here is what drove the move and what to watch next.

By FXNewsBias Team·Published Wed, 19 Aug 2026·4 min read

Swiss Franc (CHF) surges to 75/100 bullish as dollar weakness and safe-haven demand combine to lift the franc higher.

Learn why CHF outperforms on fading Fed rate-hike bets and geopolitical risk-off, and what NZD/CHF traders must watch next.

What Happened

The Swiss Franc strengthened decisively during the London session as two powerful tailwinds aligned in the franc's favour. First, the US Dollar Index weakened on fading Fed rate hike expectations ahead of the FOMC Minutes release, removing a key headwind that has pressured CHF/USD pairs in recent weeks. FXStreet reported that the franc gained ground specifically as the dollar fell on these diminished rate-hike bets, a direct reversal of the hawkish dollar narrative that dominated earlier in the month.

Second, geopolitical tensions at the Hormuz Strait triggered a broad safe-haven bid across the forex market, with the Swiss Franc among the primary beneficiaries. Risk-averse flows lifted CHF pairs across the board as traders rotated out of higher-yielding and cyclical currencies into the franc's traditional shelter status. This dual driver, monetary policy repricing plus genuine geopolitical unease, created a powerful backdrop for franc strength that overrode technical resistance and consolidated the currency's bullish setup.

The combination pushed CHF sentiment to 75/100, making it the session's biggest mover and signalling conviction among market participants that the franc's recovery has legs. With oil extending gains to a fourth day on the Hormuz situation, the risk-off environment appears sticky rather than transitory, supporting sustained franc demand.

Market Reaction

The broader forex market reflected a clear risk-off tilt, with sentiment heavily skewed toward safe-haven currencies and away from yield-sensitive alternatives. The widest sentiment gap emerged between CHF at 75/100 bullish and NZD at 32/100 bearish, a staggering 43-point spread that encapsulates the session's flight-to-safety dynamic. NZD/CHF, the key pair to watch, became a direct proxy for this divergence as the franc rallied while the Kiwi fell victim to both geopolitical headwinds and the unwinding of carry-trade positioning.

Concurrently, the Japanese Yen and Canadian Dollar both posted bullish 72/100 scores, driven partly by overlapping USD weakness and partly by domestic central bank narratives. The sterling gained on UK inflation expectations, the euro held above 1.1550 on dollar softness, and the Australian Dollar retreated despite hawkish RBA commentary, a clear sign that risk sentiment, not domestic data strength, dominated currency pair price action during the London window.

What's Driving the Move

Three key threads run through the bullish Swiss Franc story:

  1. FXStreet reported that the US Dollar Index weakened on fading Fed rate hike expectations ahead of the FOMC Minutes, directly undermining dollar strength versus the franc.
  2. Geopolitical tensions at the Hormuz Strait triggered a safe-haven bid for CHF, with oil extending gains to a fourth consecutive day and lifting demand for traditional shelter currencies.
  3. Risk-off flows rotated capital away from higher-yielding currencies such as NZD and AUD, creating a 43-point sentiment gap between the bullish franc and bearish Kiwi that manifested in NZD/CHF downside.

What to Watch Next

📈 Bull case for the move
The release of the FOMC Minutes later in the New York session could cement a dovish narrative if the Fed signals lower-for-longer rate paths, extending CHF's rally as dollar weakness accelerates. Technical confirmation above key resistance levels, combined with sustained Hormuz tensions and further crude oil gains, would reinforce the safe-haven bid and keep franc pairs bid through the Asia open.
📉 Risk to the view
An unexpectedly hawkish FOMC Minutes or surprise hawkish Fed official comment could trigger a sharp dollar rebound, unwinding the franc's recent gains and reversing the rate-hike repricing that drove today's move. Alternatively, a rapid de-escalation of geopolitical tensions at Hormuz would evaporate the risk-off bid and allow yield-seeking flows to return, pressuring CHF pairs lower and favouring cyclical currencies once again.

Watch for the FOMC Minutes tonight and any fresh Hormuz headlines during the Asia session, as both could reshape the franc's near-term exchange rate trajectory.
