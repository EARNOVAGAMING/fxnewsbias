Market Reaction

The forex market displayed a stark divergence in sentiment, with JPY rallying while the US Dollar Index tumbled to near 101.00 following the US-Iran strike halt. USD weakness was the session's defining feature—the greenback shed safe-haven appeal as diplomacy hopes dimmed geopolitical urgency, leaving the dollar as the laggard across major pairs. This created an asymmetric setup: while GBP (65/100 bullish) and EUR (62/100 bullish) benefited from dollar weakness, JPY gained on an additional layer of positive momentum tied specifically to BoJ policy expectations and technical support.

USD/JPY emerged as the key pair to watch, anchoring between 164 and broader carry-trade dynamics. The 33-point sentiment spread between JPY (68 bullish) and USD (35 bearish) underscores how sharply the narrative has rotated against dollar strength, positioning the yen as a relative outperformer in a week where central bank messaging will dominate price action.

What's Driving the Move

Three key threads run through the bullish Japanese Yen story:

  1. Japanese Yen gains against US Dollar at the start of Fed-BoJ policy week, with markets repricing BoJ rate expectations and safe-haven demand returning on geopolitical uncertainty.

What to Watch Next

📈 Bull case for the move
A hawkish BoJ surprise—such as a rate hike or aggressive forward guidance—would confirm JPY strength and could push USD/JPY decisively lower through 163. Technical support near 164 combined with positive EUR/JPY technicals suggests that a BoJ tilt toward tighter policy could trigger cascading short covering in the dollar across the session.
📉 Risk to the view
If the BoJ takes a dovish tone or delays policy tightening, JPY momentum could evaporate quickly, allowing USD/JPY to reclaim 165+ and reverse the week's gains. A sudden escalation in US-Iran tensions or a hawkish FOMC surprise would also flip the safe-haven narrative back toward the dollar, undercutting yen strength.

Watch Asia's open for any overnight developments and monitor central bank rhetoric closely; the BoJ decision will likely set the tone for the remainder of the week's currency strength dynamics.
