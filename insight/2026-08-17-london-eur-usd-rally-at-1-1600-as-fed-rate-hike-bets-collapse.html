EUR/USD Rally at 1.1600 as Fed Rate-Hike Bets Collapse

What Happened

The euro staged a decisive rally on Monday as the US Dollar Index slumped toward 99.50, undermined by a sharp reversal in Federal Reserve rate-hike pricing. According to FXStreet, the Euro rallied to a two-month high with bulls eyeing the 1.1600 level, capitalizing on softer-than-expected US economic data that has forced traders to trim near-term tightening bets. This repricing of Fed policy expectations has been the dominant theme reshaping currency flows across the majors, with the greenback losing ground across nearly every pair.

EUR/USD itself has moved into a critical technical phase. Action Forex reported that the pair's rally is now meeting a crucial resistance barrier, with bulls closely monitoring whether price action can breach and hold above this juncture. The combination of euro strength and dollar weakness — both driven by the same macro narrative of receding US rate hikes — has created a powerful unidirectional move that has trapped many short-EUR positioning into losses and drawn fresh buy orders from technical followers.

Market Reaction

Across the broader forex market, the sentiment distribution reveals a sharp bifurcation: EUR commands 72/100 bullish sentiment while USD languishes at just 28/100, representing the widest gap among all major currency pairs. The immediate trigger has been a coordinated unwinding of dollar longs as traders pushed back Fed hike bets in response to mixed US economic signals, with the Japanese Yen (66/100 bullish), British Pound (68/100), Australian Dollar (70/100), and New Zealand Dollar (68/100) all grinding higher on the same USD weakness thesis.

EUR/USD has emerged as the flagship pair of this session, with bulls now focused on whether technical confirmation above resistance can ignite a fresh leg toward 1.1600. The wider FX session has rewarded currencies with hawkish central bank backdrop — particularly AUD on RBA strength — or those benefiting from safe-haven flows, while the greenback has become a crowded short as the narrative around imminent Fed tightening has crumbled. Volume and momentum suggest conviction behind the move, though the proximity to key technical barriers means the next 48 hours will be decisive for trend confirmation.

What's Driving the Move

Three key threads run through the bullish Euro story:

  1. Traders have aggressively repriced Federal Reserve rate-hike expectations lower following softer Q2 GDP data and mixed economic signals, causing the US Dollar Index to weaken toward 99.50 and unlocking euro strength across multiple currency pairs.
  2. EUR/USD has rallied into a critical technical barrier near 1.1600 according to Action Forex, with bulls now focused on whether sustained price action above resistance can trigger fresh buying and overcome key Fibonacci levels.
  3. Gold and other risk assets have benefited from the same dollar weakness, with bullion rallying near $4,400 as Fed hike expectations drop — a secondary confirmation that the market is repricing terminal rate assumptions rather than responding to geopolitical risk alone.

What to Watch Next

📈 Bull case for the move
A break and hold above 1.1600 in EUR/USD would confirm a fresh leg higher and likely target 1.1650–1.1700, especially if US economic data continues to disappoint and central bank speakers reinforce a patient Fed stance. Upcoming China July economic data releases could also reignite risk appetite if growth signals prove resilient, further penalizing the dollar and supporting euro strength.
📉 Risk to the view
A sudden reversal in Fed rate-cut pricing — triggered by hotter-than-expected inflation data or hawkish FOMC commentary — could snap the euro's rally and force a retracement back toward 1.1450–1.1500. Technical failure at the current resistance level would also invalidate the bullish setup and invite profit-taking from trend-following algorithmic traders.

Watch the Asia session for China's delayed July economic data, which could reignite demand-driven dollar flows and test whether the euro's two-month rally has genuine fundamental legs.
