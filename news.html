Forex News FAQ

What is forex news?

Forex news is any economic or geopolitical development that moves currency exchange rates, central-bank rate decisions (Fed, ECB, BoE, BoJ), inflation data (CPI), employment reports (NFP), GDP, PMIs and major geopolitical events. FXNewsBias aggregates these and scores their sentiment impact on the eight major currencies.

Where can I get live forex news today?

Right here. This page streams live forex news updated continuously from 16+ sources, each headline tagged with the currencies it affects and a bull or bear sentiment score, a faster read than scrolling a raw newswire.

What forex news moves the market the most?

High-impact events move price the most: central-bank rate decisions, CPI inflation, US non-farm payrolls (NFP), GDP releases and major geopolitical shocks. These are flagged “High” impact in the feed above.

How often is the forex news updated?

The headline feed refreshes continuously, and our currency sentiment scores are recalculated every few hours as fresh news arrives, so the bias you see reflects the latest market-moving stories.

Is FXNewsBias forex news free?

Yes. Live forex news and real-time currency sentiment are free to use. A Pro plan adds extras like the weekly report and instant alerts.
