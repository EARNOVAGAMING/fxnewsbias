Forecast Distribution

Stand-aside forecasts are published like any other call but excluded from win-rate calculation — declining to call a direction is information, not a result.

Methodology

Forecasts are generated every weekday at 06:13 UTC from the sentiment gap between base and quote currencies (AI-scored news flow, refreshed every 3 hours). Gaps under 15 points publish as Stand Aside. Entry price is frozen at publish; settlement occurs at the next trading day's price. Moves within ±0.15% grade as Flat.

Rows are written once and never edited — the ledger is append-only by design.
