Forex Economic Calendar

The forex economic calendar tracks the scheduled data releases and central-bank events that move currency markets — interest-rate decisions, inflation (CPI), jobs reports (NFP), GDP and the ISM/PMI surveys. What makes this calendar different is the live sentiment beside every event: each release is tagged with the current news-based bias of the currency it affects, so you can see the market's standing position before the number prints.

Use it alongside the live forex news feed, our currency sentiment and pair analysis, and the daily market insight. High-impact events (flagged red) tend to cause the sharpest volatility — beats versus forecast are typically bullish for that currency, misses bearish.

Economic Calendar FAQ

What is a forex economic calendar?

A forex economic calendar is a schedule of upcoming economic data releases and central-bank events — interest-rate decisions, inflation (CPI), jobs (NFP), GDP and more — that move currency exchange rates. FXNewsBias also tags each event with the live sentiment of the currency it affects.

Which economic events move forex the most?

The highest-impact events are central-bank rate decisions (Fed, ECB, BoE, BoJ), US Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP), CPI inflation, GDP and the ISM/PMI surveys. These are flagged “High” impact above.

What time is Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP) released?

NFP is released on the first Friday of each month at 8:30 AM US Eastern time — 12:30 UTC in summer, 13:30 UTC in winter. It is the single biggest scheduled mover for the US dollar.

How is the FXNewsBias calendar different?

Every event here is paired with the current news-based sentiment and bias of the currency it affects, so you can see the market's standing position going into each release — not just the schedule.

Is the forex economic calendar free?

Yes. The FXNewsBias economic calendar and real-time currency sentiment are free to use.
